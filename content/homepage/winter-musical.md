**THE WINTER MUSICAL IS COMING!** The Minnehaha Players will present "The Rockin' Tale of Snow White" showcasing the talents of Minnehaha UMC's kids, youth and adults. Performance dates are on the weekends of January 30 - February 1 and February 6 - 8. Mark your calendar!
