**THURSDAY MORNING PLAYGROUP FOR CHILDREN:** Children five and under - and their caregivers - are invited to spend Thursday mornings from 9:30 - 11:30 a.m. at what we call "Minnehaha Playgroup." They gather in the Minnehaha Childcare Room to play, talk, explore, support and learn together.
