**WE ARE A RECONCILING CONGREGATION:** Minnehaha United Methodist Church is a welcoming community that offers unconditional love and acceptance to all people. We are a progressive, reconciling church and serve as a central hub to support our neighbors - we house Minnehaha Food Shelf to fight food insecurity in our community.
