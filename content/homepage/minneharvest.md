**VOLUNTEER AT MINNEHARVEST FOOD MISSION:** MinneHarvest is our once-a-month neighborhood food mission. Join us on the fourth Saturday of the month at 7:30 a.m. to help set up, or later at 8:00 a.m. to help clients with food, or to get food if you need it. We'll be done by 10:30 a.m.
