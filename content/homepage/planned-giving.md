**PLANNED GIVING: FOR THE FUTURE:** Along with monetary donations, Minnehaha UMC accepts gifts of stock, bonds and other securities, as well as real estate and more. Also, consider making Minnehaha UMC a beneficiary of your life insurance and including us in your will or trust. Contact the church treasurer (finance@minnehaha.org) or the pastor for more information.
