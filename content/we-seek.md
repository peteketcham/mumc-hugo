At Minnehaha United Methodist Church, worship is not confined to one hour of the week every Sunday. It's a part of who we are...and who are are to become.

We worship God in many ways:

- Sunday services
- Taizé service (third Friday of the month at 7:00 p.m., October through May)
- Bible study
- Prayer
- Fellowship
- Food ministry (MinneHarvest, Minnehaha Food Shelf, Serving at Simpson Shelter, Meals on Wheels)
- Sunday School (for all ages)
- Exercise
- Acceptance of others
- Following the example of Jesus Christ

We encourage all people to grow in their faith and knowledge of God through our education programs and discussion groups.

**OUR MISSION:**

To make disciples of Jesus Christ for the transformation of the world.

**OUR VISION:**

We seek, we serve, we celebrate, and we carry our Christian faith into our community and the world. We provide a caring gathering place to worship God and to grow spiritually.

**OUR VALUES:**

We strive to be a community of Christian faith that lives joyously, acts boldly, leads courageously, and serves humbly.

- We are committed to equality and inclusion
- We are working to become carbon neutral
- We strive to offer competitive pay and health care for all employees
- We want to feed the hungry and help people in need

Join us in worship at Minnehaha.
