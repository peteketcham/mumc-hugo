In October of 2018, we installed two solar arrays which covers approximately 40% of energy consumption. This project was made possible by the work and support of our Minnehaha Solar Committee, Apadana Solar, the [Made in Minnesota Solar Incentive Program](https://mn.gov/commerce/industries/energy/solar/mim/) and many incredible people from our congregation.
