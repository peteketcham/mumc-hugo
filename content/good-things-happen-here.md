## A Progressive Christian Church

## A Reconciling Congregation

**Minnehaha United Methodist Church (3701 E. 50th Street) is a welcoming community that offers unconditional love and acceptance to all people. We are a progressive, reconciling church and serve as a central hub to support our neighbors – we house Minnehaha Food Shelf to fight food insecurity in our community. Good things happen here.**

## Fighting for Racial Justice and Equity

**The Racial Justice Team at Minnehaha United Methodist Church (3701 E. 50th Street) is guided by the United Methodist Church's stance against racism, by our commitment to achieve an understanding of the harms of systemic racism and by concrete actions which we can take to promote social justice. Good things happen here.**

## Land Acknowledgement Statement

**Minnehaha United Methodist Church (3701 E. 50th Street) recognizes that the land upon which the church is built – as well as the surrounding land – holds spiritual, historical, and personal significance to Dakota People, the first people of this land.**
