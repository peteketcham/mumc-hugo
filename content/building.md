Fill out the form and submit it to the MUMC Church Secretary.

## Gym Rules & Liability Waiver

**Keep the Gym Looking Good!**

- Keep gym clean and picked-up
- No heavy carts or equipment
- No skates or scooters
- No food, gum or liquids – except water with a cap or cover
- Gaffer or painting tape only
- Use gym at your own risk
