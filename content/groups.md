Minnehaha UMC is home to a wide range of groups and organizations. Some are part of our church life, and others are community groups that meet in our building.

## Church groups

- **[United Women of Faith](/uwf/)** welcomes women of any age or interest to grow in spirit and support missions in the neighborhood and around the world.
- **[Racial Justice](/racial-justice/)** is our response to the call of our Christian faith to love our neighbor and work for justice. Visit the page for books and resources we recommend.
- **[Youth Ministries](/youth/)** offers teens and tweens great opportunities to know God as we seek, serve and celebrate.
- **[Children, Sunday School and Playgroup](/kids/)** has programs for kids and their families all year long.
- **Chancel Choir** enriches our traditional service with hymns, piano and organ music. See [Worship](/we-seek/).
- **Bible study** and discussion groups help all people grow in their faith and knowledge of God.
- **[Food ministries](/food-ministries/)**: MinneHarvest, the Minnehaha Food Shelf and Meals on Wheels all rely on volunteers.

## Groups that meet here

Our building is also home to Scouts (Troop 1), Fun and Fitness (exercise), Taleweavers Toastmasters, Overeaters Anonymous, Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) and more.

To check meeting days and times, see the [calendar](/calendar/) or [contact the church office](/contact/).
